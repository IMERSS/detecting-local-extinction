In historical habitat, the inputs to the first analysis stage which infers 
a per-cell posterior distribution for extirpation probability are just the 
gridded per-cell search effort and (Solow) prior extirpation probability.

In the following two pages we illustrate the inference process in 
historical habitat for the two focal
taxa we've treated in previous sections, _Primula pauciflora_ and _Crassula connata_.
