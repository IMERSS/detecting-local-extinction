# Protocol for the Detection of Extirpation

Developed on Galiano Island, this protocol for detecting local extinction, or extirpation, combines historical
specimens, community science observations and targeted searches. Weighing the search effort spent in each species'
historical and potential habitat, the protocol tests the hypothesis that a population has been lost, in line with
IUCN extinction criteria.
Published in [*Plants, People, Planet*](https://nph.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/ppp3.70130).
